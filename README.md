### Projects

**[Is gold still a safe haven? Bitcoin and gold in the 2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis](https://github.com/betterandy24/bitcoin-gold-safe-haven)** · Python
Gold's beta to the S&P 500 rose from 0.08 (2019–2025) to about 1.0 during the conflict, higher than in any of six earlier market shocks since 2019. Bitcoin kept behaving like a risky asset, not "digital gold".
`Baur–Lucey regressions` `factor models with real rates` `rolling betas` `event study`

**[Did Canada's 2024 surtax on Chinese steel shift where Canada buys its steel?](https://github.com/betterandy24/canada-steel-surtax)** · R
China's import share fell by about 2 percentage points more for targeted products than for untargeted ones, and Japan was the most likely gainer. The evidence is suggestive rather than conclusive: only four months separate the surtax from the 2025 US–Canada trade dispute.
`difference-in-differences` `product-level panel` `event study` `clustered & Newey-West SEs`
